About

Hey, I'm Yaspieze.

Yaspz, if that's easier. I make nerdcore, mostly alone, usually late.

What that means

Nerdcore is songs about games and shows, and usually about the characters stuck inside them. Poppy Playtime, Bendy and the Ink Machine, The Amazing Digital Circus, Verity, Minecraft.

I write it, sing it, arrange it and mix it. Nobody else touches it, which is exactly why there isn't a new song every week. I'd rather it be right than fast.

First upload went up in early 2023. New music most months, a short video in between when a full song isn't ready.
