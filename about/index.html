01 / About me

It starts with
a story.

Yaspz is a nerdcore artist who began experimenting with music in 2026. Drawing inspiration from games and shows of all kinds, she turns the characters and stories she loves into original songs.

She handles each stage of her music herself—from writing and singing to arranging, producing, and mixing. Her sound can change from song to song, and she continues to experiment and create new music.
