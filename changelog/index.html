Changelog

What changed, and when.

Every change to this site, newest first. Mostly so I remember what I broke.

  1. 29 October 2026

    Five pages instead of eight Layout

    The figure and the collaborators were pages of their own with very little on them. They live inside About now, where anyone reading about me would look anyway.

    • Home, About, Listen, Contact, Changelog. That's the whole site.
    • Merch left for its own shop at store.fngl.ir. The button in the corner goes straight there.
    • Copy cut everywhere. Shorter sentences, fewer of them, same amount said.
  2. 29 October 2026

    Colours that match her Look

    The site was brown and it clashed with the character. It's plum and lilac now, and the four seasons shift around that instead of running off to their own colours.

    • Deeper accent colour, softer glow behind the panels.
    • Paper grain over the whole page and a wobbly hand-drawn line under headings, so it reads less like a template.
    • Dark mode rebalanced so the plum still shows up against black.
  3. 29 October 2026

    Locked the page down Behind it

    • The page is only allowed to load things from itself. No third-party scripts, no remote fonts.
    • Each page carries its own list of the only scripts allowed to run, written automatically when the site is built so it can never fall out of date.
    • Off-site links no longer pass along the page you came from.
  4. 29 October 2026

    Wrote it like a person Words

    Read the whole site out loud and hated most of it. The worst habit was saying one thing three times in slightly different words on three different pages.

    • Every page rewritten. Slogans that didn't say anything are gone.
    • Small places too: the 404, the copy confirmation, the line above the video.
  5. 28 October 2026

    Fixed the things that were quietly broken Access

    • Grey text on cream was too pale. Darker now, still grey.
    • Buttons with only a white outline nearly vanished on paper. They've got an edge.
    • The play button told your eyes one thing and a screen reader another. They match now.
    • Product photos load as images instead of being baked into the page. It went from 3.4 MB to a fraction of that.
  6. 27 October 2026

    Put the glass back Look

    There was a stretch where the site went flat and paper-coloured and I didn't like it. The frosted panels came back, with colour behind them.

    • New homepage top: wordmark big, one line from me, two buttons.
    • Contact rebuilt around the Discord ID with a copy button, because typing it by hand is miserable.
    • Blur only where it earns its keep. Everywhere else it's a gradient and a rim, which looks the same and doesn't crawl.

Older than this
