How ordering works

Nothing gets charged here. You build the order, the cart writes it out, and I come back with postage and a total before anyone pays anything.

  1. Pick the piece and the size. Each size button travels with the order, so I know what to pack.
  2. Press copy in the cart. It gives you the list and an order tag to paste straight into Discord.
  3. I come back with a total. Stock, postage and the price, before anything is paid.
  4. You pay, I post. Then the piece is out of my hands and into the post.

Sizes and the sale

Every piece of clothing runs S to XXL, and the same range applies to the tees and the hoodies. The code takes 15% off the pieces; postage is quoted separately with the total, because it depends where it is going.

Out of season?

The beanie and the mug go away when it warms up. The tote goes away when it gets cold. They come back with the weather instead of sitting here looking silly, and anything already in your cart stays in your cart.
