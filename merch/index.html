How ordering works

Nothing is charged on this site. The cart writes the order out for you, and I quote postage and confirm stock before you pay a penny.

  1. Pick the piece and the size. Each size button travels with the order, so I know what to pack.
  2. Press copy in the cart. It gives you the list and an order tag to paste straight into Discord.
  3. I come back with a total. Stock, postage and the price, before anything is paid.
  4. You pay, I post. Then the piece is out of my hands and into the post.

Sizes and the sale

Every piece runs S to XXL, and the same range applies to the tees and the hoodies. The autumn code takes 15% off the pieces; postage is quoted separately with the total, because it depends where it is going.
