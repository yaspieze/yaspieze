Using my
music.

Want to include a Yaspieze song in something you’re making? Here’s what you can share, how to credit it, and when to ask first.

The short version

My music isn’t generally free to reuse. You can use it in the specific ways below if you follow the credit and non-monetization requirements. For anything else, please contact me first.

What you can share

  • Short videos: You may use an official in-app sound, or your own version of the audio, in non-monetized short videos. Give clear credit to Yaspieze, name the song, and link to the original upload or channel where the platform allows it.
  • Reactions, reviews, and commentary on YouTube: You may include clips or the full song as part of a genuine non-monetized reaction, review, or commentary video. Clearly credit Yaspieze, name the song, and link to the original upload or channel in your title or description. Your video must add your own commentary and must not serve as a replacement for the original.

Official platform sound libraries may impose their own rules, including restrictions on business accounts. Please follow the platform’s terms too.

How to give credit

Make the credit easy to find. Include the artist name, the correct song title, and a link to the original video or the Yaspieze channel. Replace the bracketed text below with the actual song details:

Music: Yaspieze, [song title]
Original video or channel: [link]

On a platform that does not support clickable links, include the name and title, and provide the link wherever the platform allows. Credit alone does not grant permission for uses outside the guidelines on this page.

Please ask first

  • Monetization and commercial uses: Get written permission before earning revenue from content using my music, claiming or distributing its revenue, or using it in advertisements, sponsorships, paid promotions, or other commercial projects.
  • Covers, remixes, and instrumentals: Please request written permission before publishing a cover or remix, recording over an instrumental, or using official or unofficial instrumentals in a project.
  • Other uses: If your project doesn’t fit the allowances above, ask before using the music.

What’s not allowed without permission

  • Reuploading or distributing a full track, extended version, or music upload as a substitute for the original, including on topic channels, sound libraries, and distribution services.
  • Claiming the music as your own, changing the artist credit, or presenting altered audio, artwork, or visuals as an original release by someone else.
  • Publishing remixes, slowed, sped-up, looped, or otherwise reworked tracks as standalone music uploads. The short-video allowance above does not permit re-releasing a song as your own.

Ask about a project

For permission or licensing questions, contact yappieze on Discord. Please include the song title, how and where you plan to use it, whether the project is monetized, and a link to your channel or project if available.

These guidelines describe permission from Yaspieze only. A project may also require permission from other rights holders, and platform rules still apply. Nothing here is intended to limit rights provided by applicable law.
