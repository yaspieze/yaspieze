About Me

Yaspieze is a nerdcore artist creating original songs inspired by games and animated shows.

Latest Music

Yaspz is a nerdcore artist who started experimenting with music in 2026. Inspired by games and shows of all kinds, she creates songs about the characters and stories she loves.

She handles every part of her music herself, from writing and singing to arranging, producing, and mixing. Her sound changes with each project as she continues to experiment and make new music.
