About

Hey, I'm Yaspieze. Yaspz, if that's easier.

I make nerdcore. Most of it starts with something I've been watching or playing. Poppy Playtime, Bendy and the Ink Machine, The Amazing Digital Circus, Verity, Minecraft. I take the mood of whatever it is and build a song around it.

I write it, sing it, arrange it and mix it myself, which is why there's never as much of it as I'd like.
