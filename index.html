A little more about me and my music.

What is nerdcore?

Nerdcore is music inspired by things like games, animation, characters, and fandoms. For me, it’s a way to turn the stories I love into songs.

Where can I listen?

You can find the videos and music on the Yaspieze YouTube channel. You can also listen to my latest video right here.

How do I keep up with new music?

Subscribe on YouTube to see new uploads as they arrive. There’s no fixed release schedule.

Are collaborations open?

For nerdcore music collaborations, you can reach out on Discord at yappieze.
