Questions?Answered.

The essentials, briefly.

Is the music free to listen to?+

Yes. Every video on the channel is free to watch on YouTube — no subscription, no paywall, nothing locked away.

What is nerdcore?+

Music about the things fans love: games, anime, internet culture and the stories in between. Think of it as a love letter to fandom, with a beat.

What are the songs about?+

Characters, worlds and moments from gaming and animation — familiar stories heard from a different perspective.

How often are there new uploads?+

New worlds land regularly. Subscribe and turn on notifications so you never miss an upload.

Can I request a song?+

Absolutely — requests and ideas from listeners shape what comes next. Drop yours in the comments on YouTube.

Where else can I find Yaspieze?+

YouTube is home. Everything starts there, and the about page has whatever else you need.
