What to send me

Tell me what you're working on, send a link to something you've made, and say roughly when it should come out. That's enough for me to know whether it fits.

  • A link to finished work, not a pitch on its own.
  • Your rough deadline, even if it's a guess.
  • What you want me for. Vocals, a verse, a whole track.

Anything that isn't a collab pitch is still fine. I read everything, even when I'm slow to answer.
